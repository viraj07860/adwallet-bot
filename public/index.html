Help & FAQ
Answers to common questions about earning and withdrawals.
How do I earn on AdWallet? ▾
Earn by watching rewarded ads, claiming your daily bonus, referring friends, and sending users to offer links you configure.
What is the minimum withdrawal? ▾
The minimum withdrawal is $100.00. Once you reach that amount, you can request a payout using one of the available cashout methods.
How does the referral system work? ▾
Share your referral link. When someone joins through your link and watches their first ad, your referral reward is credited automatically.
How do VIP plans work? ▾
VIP plans increase your reward multiplier. You can activate VIP through Telegram Stars, crypto payment with proof submission, or referral milestones.
I paid with crypto. What next? ▾
Use the VIP crypto flow, send the exact amount, then tap the screenshot button and upload your payment proof in the Telegram bot chat for manual review.
